# Storylines

**Built: A. Into the pocket.** The one a stranger follows: the product's own promise, acted out.

## A. Into the pocket (built)

One continuous take across five worlds. The desktop fills the frame, finishes a build, and the camera
pulls back until it is a phone's screen; a thumb drives it; the phone turns beside a laptop and a text and
a file cross; the phone leaves home on a map; a deploy waits for a yes from the phone; the phone becomes
the brand's window and drops into the pocket.
- First two seconds: a full desktop, a terminal typing `cargo build --release`.
- Beats: desktop → into the phone → drive it → both ways → anywhere → your scripts → the mark.
- Look: the site's own components on a navy studio sweep. Sound: keys, taps, whooshes on moves, an impact
  on the mark.

## B. A day away from the desk

A moment in someone's day, cut on time stamps: 08:40 a build finishes at home and the phone says so; 12:15
at a café the phone drives the desktop over the internet; 18:30 on a train a text is answered from the
laptop's toast; 23:00 the lost phone rings from the tray.

## C. The type does the arguing

Giant kinetic type on the night wallpaper; each phrase turns into its object: "your desktop" shrinks into
a phone, "both ways" becomes two items crossing, "anywhere" draws the road through both routers, "your
pocket" becomes the mark.
